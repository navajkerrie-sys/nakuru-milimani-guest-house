A house not a hotel chain

Marula House opened in 2014 on land that once belonged to a coffee farm. We kept the old stone stores and the row of marula trees that give the house its name, and built twelve rooms around them, each one different from the next.

The Rooms

The Acacia Sunctuary Suite

A serene retreat designed for deep comfort and quiet luxury. Featuring low-profile bedding, ambient wall lighting, and a plush lounge set on warm herringbone wood flooring. Floor-to-ceiling valley views bring the Rift landscape inside, while the seclude inner courtyard ensures complete privacy and calm.

Reserve

The Rift Panaroma Suite

Our signature suite where contemporary luxury meets panaromic valley views. Dressed in rich wood tones, premium linen, and a spacious L-shaped lounge with a dedicated workspace. Expansive windows and soft layered lighting create an atmosphere of effortless comfort and refined elegance. Reserve

The Baobab Exclusive Loft

An elevated loft-style retreat crafted for refined comfort and warmth. Featuring a rich timber feature wall, premium bedding with bespoke accents, and soft herringbone flooring. Intimate ambient lighting and plush seating create a cozy, upscale atmosphere perfect for both relaxation and extended stays.

Reserve

The Marula Signature Suite

Our most distinguished suite, defined by its soaring vaulted timber ceiling and full valley elegance; A spacious haven with a private lounge, generous sleeping quarters, and a secluded veranda for sunset views. Warm wooden finishes and soft, layered lighting deliver ultimate luxury, space, and tranquility.
